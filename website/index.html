OUR HERITAGE & MISSION

BORN IN THE INDUSTRIAL HEART OF MANCHESTER

Founded in 2016 by Head Coach Sean Krool, Fight Factory Manchester was built on one principle: authentic combat training without corporate gimmicks or ego.

Located at Gate 3, Unit 16B Lord North Street, our raw industrial facility brings together 25+ years of world championship level boxing expertise. Whether you're stepping into a boxing ring for the first time or preparing for professional fight night, we forge physical endurance and mental fortitude.

World Class Coaching

Sean Krool has prepared elite world champions, including training work with Usyk, Crolla & Hatton camps.

Zero Ego Culture

Every member gets respected regardless of experience level. First-timers receive full hands-on guidance.
