Questions?

Do I need to book to visit?+
No. Walk in anytime during opening hours. Your first session is completely free.
What are your opening hours?+
Mon–Fri: 5PM–8:30PM. Saturday: 9AM–12PM. Members get extended access.
Do you offer personal training?+
Yes. 1-to-1 sessions with Sean Krool at £40/session. Block bookings available.
Can I cancel my membership?+
Yes. No contracts. Cancel anytime with no fees. You just won't be charged the following month.
Do you have junior classes?+
Yes. Junior Boxing Club for ages 8–16, Saturdays 11AM–12PM. Safe, structured, and fun.
What should I bring to my first class?+
Just comfortable workout clothes and water. We have gloves and wraps you can borrow.
